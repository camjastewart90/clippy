# Getting Started with AI Clippy 📎

Welcome! AI Clippy is a friendly paperclip that floats on your Mac, answers questions, can **look at your screen** to help, and can do a few handy things for you. Here's how to get going in about 3 minutes.

---

## 1. Install it

1. Open the **`AI Clippy-…-arm64.dmg`** file you were sent.
2. Drag the **AI Clippy** icon into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open **Applications** and double-click **AI Clippy**.

It opens without any security warnings. Clippy appears in the **bottom-right corner** of your screen — click him to open the chat.

> **Mac type:** this version runs on **Apple Silicon Macs** (M1/M2/M3/M4 — most Macs from 2020 on). It won't run on older Intel Macs.

---

## 2. Give Clippy a brain (pick a model)

Clippy needs an AI model to talk to. On first launch he opens to **Settings** (the ⚙️ gear). Pick one:

### Option A — Free & easy (recommended): Google Gemini
1. Go to **[aistudio.google.com/apikey](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey)** and sign in with a Google account.
2. Click **Create API key** and **copy** it. (Free — no credit card. ~1,500 messages/day.)
3. In Clippy's ⚙️ Settings: set **Provider → Google Gemini**, paste the key, click **Test connection** (the dot goes green), then **Save**.

That's it — start chatting!

### Option B — Also free & fast: Groq
Same steps, but get a key from **[console.groq.com/keys](https://console.groq.com/keys)** and choose **Provider → Groq**.

### Option C — Fully private / offline: LM Studio
Runs the AI on your own Mac, nothing leaves the machine. Install **[LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai)**, download a **vision** model, click **Start Server**, then choose **Provider → LM Studio** in Clippy. (Slower, but private.)

---

## 3. Using Clippy

- **Ask anything:** click Clippy, type in the notepad, press **Enter**.
- **👀 Look at my screen:** attaches a screenshot so Clippy can help with what you're doing.
- **▢ Region:** drag-select just part of your screen to ask about.
- **📎 File:** open a PDF, image, or text file and ask about it ("summarise this").
- **Global shortcuts:**
  - **⌘⇧Space** anywhere → Clippy instantly looks at your screen and answers.
  - **⌘⇧E** → act on whatever **text you've highlighted** in any app (summarise / explain / rewrite / translate).
- **🧠 Memory:** Clippy remembers facts about you across sessions (tell him "remember my name is…").
- **Actions:** ask him to *do* things ("open google.com", "make a note of…", "copy this") — he'll show an **Approve / Dismiss** card first. Nothing happens without your OK.
- **🎵 Music** and **🎮 Games** (Pong / Space Invaders) in the header for when you're waiting.
- **Move & resize:** drag Clippy anywhere; drag the panel's top-left corner to resize.

---

## 4. Permissions (only when you use those features)

macOS will ask permission the first time you use certain features — grant them to **"AI Clippy"**:

- **Screen Recording** — for "Look at my screen" / region capture. (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording → turn on **AI Clippy**, then reopen the app.)
- **Accessibility** — only for the "type text" action and the ⌘⇧E selection shortcut.

These are one-time and normal.

---

## A note on privacy

- With **Gemini/Groq** (cloud), your messages and any screenshots you send go to that provider, and their free tiers may use your data to improve their models — so avoid sending anything sensitive.
- With **LM Studio** (local), nothing ever leaves your Mac.
- Screenshots are only ever sent **when you choose** (the screen/region/hotkey buttons) — never in the background.

Enjoy! It looks like you're getting started with a helpful paperclip. 📎
